Core Vocabulary

A spelling game for two, hosted by Wormsworth. He lives in an apple and has read the dictionary twice.

  1. Wormsworth says a word.
  2. One of you spells it out loud.
  3. The other types exactly what they hear and presses Enter.

Right answers bring harder words. A few misses ease things up. 800 words on eight levels, from garden to scherenschnitte.
